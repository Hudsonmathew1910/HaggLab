# Green Field Sports Arena — Booking Rules and Prices

Fictional sample venue · For demonstration only

## Facilities
- Turf A and Turf B: 5-a-side football, 40 x 20 m, artificial grass, floodlit.
- Turf C: 7-a-side football, 60 x 35 m, artificial grass, floodlit.
- Badminton Court 1 and Court 2: indoor, wooden floor, air-cooled.
- Changing rooms with lockers and showers. Free drinking water. Free parking for 100 cars.

## Opening hours
Every day from 6:00 to 23:00, including public holidays. The last booking starts at 22:00.
Each booking is one hour long. You can book up to 3 hours in a row for the same court.

## Prices per hour
| Facility | Off-peak (Mon-Fri, 6:00-17:00) | Peak (Mon-Fri after 17:00, all weekend) |
|---|---|---|
| Turf A or Turf B (5-a-side) | 60 | 78 |
| Turf C (7-a-side) | 90 | 117 |
| Badminton court | 15 | 19.50 |

Equipment rental: football bibs and ball 5 per booking; badminton rackets and shuttles 3 per person.

## How to book
- Book online, by phone, or at the front desk. Online booking is open 14 days in advance.
- Pay in full online when you book. At the desk you can pay by card, UPI or cash.
- You get a confirmation email with a QR code. Show it at the front desk to check in.
- Bookings are first come, first served. A slot shown as free online is free at that moment.

## Cancellation and changes
- Cancel more than 24 hours before the start time: full refund.
- Cancel 6 to 24 hours before: 50% refund, or move the booking to another date for free.
- Cancel less than 6 hours before, or do not show up: no refund.
- If rain makes a turf unsafe, we cancel and give a full refund or a free new booking.
- Changes to the time or court are free if made more than 24 hours ahead and the new slot is free.

## Rules on the field
- Football boots with moulded studs or turf shoes only. No metal studs.
- Arrive 10 minutes early. Bookings end on time so the next group can start.
- No food, glass bottles or chewing gum on the turf or courts.
- Children under 14 must be with an adult.
- Players are responsible for their own belongings. Use the lockers.

## Regular groups and events
- Weekly fixed slots for teams and companies: book 8 weeks or more and get 10% off.
- Tournaments and corporate events: contact the front desk at least 3 weeks ahead.
