# CareFirst Family Clinic — Frequently Asked Questions

Fictional sample clinic · For demonstration only. The assistant answers practical questions about the clinic. It does not give medical advice.

## Opening hours
**When are you open?** Monday to Friday 8:00 to 20:00, Saturday 9:00 to 14:00. Closed on Sundays and public holidays.
**Are you open on public holidays?** No. The holiday list is on our website each January.
**When is the lab open?** Blood samples are taken from 8:00 to 11:00, Monday to Saturday.

## Appointments
**How do I book?** Online on our website, by phone during opening hours, or at the reception desk.
**Can I walk in without an appointment?** Yes, for general consultations, wound dressing, stitch removal and blood pressure checks. Walk-ins are seen when a slot is free; there may be a wait at busy times.
**How do I cancel or move an appointment?** Use the link in your confirmation message, or call reception. Please give at least 4 hours' notice.
**What if I am late?** If you are more than 15 minutes late we may need to move you to the next free slot.
**Can I choose my doctor?** Yes, when booking online or by phone, subject to availability.
**Do you do home visits?** We offer home blood sample collection (book by 18:00 the day before). We do not offer home doctor visits.

## Services
**What services do you offer?** General consultations, child health checks, vaccinations, lab tests, health check packages, minor procedures such as wound dressing and stitch removal, ECG, diet consultations and physiotherapy. Full list and fees: services.csv.
**Do you handle emergencies?** No. For emergencies, call the national emergency number or go to the nearest hospital emergency department.
**Do you do X-rays or scans?** No. We can refer you to a partner imaging centre.
**Do you give medical certificates?** Yes, after a consultation.

## Tests and reports
**Do I need to fast before a blood test?** Fasting for 10 hours is needed for blood sugar and cholesterol tests. Water is fine. Other tests usually need no fasting; check services.csv or ask reception.
**When are reports ready?** Most blood test reports are ready the same day after 18:00 and are sent by email. Some special tests take 2 to 3 days.
**Can someone else collect my report?** Yes, with a signed note from you and their photo ID.

## Fees, payment and insurance
**How much is a consultation?** A general consultation is 500. A follow-up within 7 days is free.
**How can I pay?** Card, UPI or cash.
**Do you accept insurance?** Most major insurance cards are accepted for consultations and tests. Bring your insurance card and ID to the visit. Some packages are not covered; reception can check before your visit.

## Getting here
**Where are you?** Ground floor of the City Square shopping complex, next to the pharmacy.
**Is there parking?** Yes, free parking for 2 hours in the complex basement.
**Is the clinic accessible?** Yes, step-free entry, a lift, and an accessible toilet.

## Children
**Do you see children?** Yes. Child health checks and vaccinations are by appointment. Please bring the vaccination card.

## Contact and messages
**How do I reach a person after hours?** Leave a message with this assistant or on our website. Reception calls back the next working morning. For anything urgent or medical, call the emergency number.
