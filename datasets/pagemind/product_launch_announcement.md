# Brightcart launches same-day delivery in three cities

Fictional sample press release · For demonstration only

Brightcart, an online store for home-office and audio products, today launched same-day delivery in three cities: Riverside, Harbour Bay and Hillview. Orders placed before 13:00 on weekdays will arrive by 21:00 the same day.

Same-day delivery costs 7.99, or is free for orders over 120. It covers about 900 of Brightcart's 1,400 products; large furniture such as desks and chairs is not included. Customers will see a "Same day" label on eligible products at checkout.

The service runs through a new partnership with a local courier network and a small warehouse opened in Harbour Bay in September. Brightcart says the warehouse holds three days of stock for its best-selling items and is restocked every night.

"Most of our customers buy because something broke or they start a new job tomorrow," said the company's operations lead. "Same-day delivery fixes the problem they actually have."

During a two-month trial in Riverside, 18% of eligible orders chose same-day delivery and 97% arrived on time. Late deliveries were refunded automatically.

Brightcart plans to add two more cities in the first half of next year if on-time delivery stays above 95%. Standard delivery, which takes 3 to 5 working days and is free over 50, does not change.
