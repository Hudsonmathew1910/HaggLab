# Northwind updates its hybrid work policy for 2027

Fictional sample article · For demonstration only

Northwind Supplies will change its hybrid work rules from 1 January 2027, the company's HR team announced this week. The update follows a staff survey in August in which 412 of 530 employees took part.

Under the new policy, employees may work from home up to three days a week instead of two. Teams will agree on two shared "anchor days" each week when everyone comes to the office, so that meetings, training and onboarding can happen in person. New employees will still work fully from the office for their first four weeks.

The survey found that 71% of staff wanted more remote days, mainly to cut commuting time. Managers, however, said that onboarding and problem-solving worked better in person. The anchor-day model is meant to balance both views.

Two other changes come with the policy. The one-time home-office allowance rises from 250 to 400 and becomes available after three months instead of after probation. And the company will pay for co-working desks for employees who live more than 40 km from an office, for up to four days a month.

Some limits stay the same. Remote days still cannot be carried over to the next week, the company VPN remains mandatory outside the office, and customer-facing teams in the warehouse and showroom are not covered by the hybrid policy.

HR will hold three question-and-answer sessions in November and publish the full policy on the HR portal by 1 December. Managers must agree the anchor days for their teams by 15 December.
