# Northwind Supplies — Staff Handbook

Version 3.2 · Fictional sample company · For demonstration only

## 1. Welcome and how to use this handbook
This handbook explains how we work at Northwind Supplies: hours, leave, pay, expenses, equipment, conduct and who to contact. It applies to all permanent employees. Contractors follow their contract first and this handbook only where the contract says so.
If anything in this handbook is unclear, ask your manager or raise a question on the HR portal. HR reviews the handbook every January and publishes changes on the HR portal.

## 2. Working hours
Office hours are 9:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday, with a one-hour lunch break between 13:00 and 14:00.
With your manager's agreement you may start any time between 8:00 and 10:00, as long as you work 8 hours a day and attend team meetings.
Core hours, when everyone should be available, are 10:30 to 16:00.
Work on Saturdays or Sundays needs written approval from your manager in advance and is paid as overtime (see section 6).

## 3. Attendance and punctuality
Record your start and end time in the attendance app every working day.
If you will be more than 30 minutes late, message your manager before your usual start time.
Three unexplained late arrivals in one month lead to a conversation with your manager; repeated lateness can lead to a formal warning.

## 4. Annual leave
Full-time employees get 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees get leave in proportion to their contracted hours.
New employees earn leave from their first day at 1.5 days per full month worked.
Request leave at least 7 days in advance on the HR portal. Requests of 5 or more working days in a row need 30 days' notice.
You can carry over up to 5 unused days to the next year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March or they lapse.
Leave cannot be taken during the annual stock count in the last week of March, except in an emergency.

## 5. Sick leave and other leave
You get 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave does not carry over.
Tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day you are ill. For absences of more than 2 working days in a row, upload a doctor's note to the HR portal when you return.
Bereavement leave: up to 5 paid days for the death of a close family member.
Parental leave: 26 weeks paid for the primary carer and 2 weeks paid for the secondary carer, after 6 months of service.
Marriage leave: 3 paid days, to be taken within 3 months of the wedding.
Jury duty and voting: paid time off as required by law.

## 6. Pay and overtime
Salaries are paid on the last working day of each month into your bank account. Payslips are on the HR portal.
Overtime applies only to approved work beyond 8 hours a day or on weekends. It is paid at 1.5 times the hourly rate, or 2 times on public holidays.
Overtime must be logged in the attendance app within 3 days and approved by your manager.
Salary reviews happen once a year in April, based on performance and company results.

## 7. Public holidays
The company observes 10 public holidays a year. The list is published on the HR portal every January.
If you work on a public holiday, you get a replacement day off, to be taken within 30 days, plus overtime pay.

## 8. Remote and hybrid work
You may work from home up to 2 days a week, agreed with your manager. Remote days do not carry over to the next week.
New employees work fully from the office for their first 4 weeks.
When working remotely: be reachable during core hours, keep your camera on for team meetings, and use the company VPN.
A one-time home-office allowance of 250 is available after probation for a chair, desk or monitor. Submit the receipt as an expense.

## 9. Expenses and travel
Business expenses are reimbursed when submitted on the expense app with receipts within 30 days of the purchase.
Any single expense above 5,000 needs your manager's approval before you spend the money.
Travel: book economy train or air travel through the travel desk at least 7 days ahead where possible.
Daily allowance during business travel: meals up to 800 per day; hotels up to 4,500 per night in large cities and 3,000 elsewhere.
Taxis are reimbursed for business trips and for travel after 21:00. Personal entertainment, fines and alcohol are not reimbursed.

## 10. Equipment and IT
Each employee receives a laptop, headset and access card. Report lost or damaged equipment to IT within 24 hours.
Do not install personal or unlicensed software on company laptops. Request software through the IT helpdesk.
Use a password manager and turn on two-factor authentication for all work accounts. Never share your password, including with IT.
Lock your screen whenever you leave your desk. Laptops must not be left in cars.
Return all equipment on your last working day.

## 11. Data protection and confidentiality
Customer and employee information is confidential. Share it only with colleagues who need it for their work.
Do not send customer data to personal email addresses or upload it to tools that IT has not approved, including public AI chatbots.
Report suspected data leaks, phishing emails or lost devices to it-security immediately, even outside working hours.

## 12. Code of conduct
Treat colleagues, customers and suppliers with respect. Harassment, bullying and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated and lead to disciplinary action.
Gifts from suppliers above a value of 1,000 must be declared to your manager. Cash gifts must never be accepted.
Conflicts of interest, such as a family member working for a supplier, must be declared on the HR portal.
Social media: do not speak on behalf of the company unless you are authorised. Do not share confidential information online.

## 13. Health and safety
Know your nearest fire exit and assembly point (the car park by Gate 2).
First-aid kits are at reception and in each kitchen. The list of trained first-aiders is on the notice board.
Report any accident or near miss to your manager and on the HR portal on the same day.

## 14. Performance and probation
Probation lasts 6 months. Your manager reviews your progress at 3 months and at the end of probation.
After probation, performance reviews happen twice a year, in April and October.
Training: each employee has a learning budget of 15,000 per year for courses and books, approved by their manager.

## 15. Grievances and disciplinary process
If you have a concern about your work or a colleague, talk to your manager first. If that is not possible, contact HR directly.
Formal grievances are answered in writing within 10 working days.
The disciplinary process has three stages: a verbal warning, a written warning and a final written warning. Serious misconduct, such as theft or violence, can lead to dismissal without warnings.

## 16. Leaving the company
The notice period is 30 days during probation and 60 days after confirmation.
Resign in writing to your manager and HR. Unused annual leave is paid in the final settlement; leave taken beyond what you earned is deducted.
You will get an exit interview and an experience letter within 7 days of your last day.

## 17. Contacts
HR questions: the HR desk on the HR portal, or hr@northwind.example.
IT problems: the IT helpdesk portal, or extension 2200.
Security incidents: it-security@northwind.example, any time.
Payroll: payroll@northwind.example.
