# Brightcart Online Store — Customer Support Policy

Fictional sample store · For demonstration only

## 1. Urgency rules
- **Urgent:** damaged or wrong item; payment taken twice; order not arrived 7 or more days after dispatch; the customer mentions a deadline within 3 days; any safety issue (overheating, burning smell, sharp edges, electric shock).
- **Normal:** returns and exchanges, address changes before dispatch, failed payments, login problems, product questions linked to an order, items out of stock.
- **Low:** general questions answered on the website, feedback, compliments, account settings.
- Urgent messages are answered within 1 hour during support hours. Normal within 4 hours. Low within 1 working day.

## 2. Topics
Returns, Delivery, Payment, Account, Product question, Other.

## 3. Returns and exchanges
- Unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 14 days of delivery.
- Damaged or wrong items: send a replacement or give a full refund, the customer's choice. A photo is enough; the customer does not need to send the damaged item back first.
- Exchanges for another size or colour are free within 14 days.
- Refunds reach the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days after we receive the return.
- Not returnable: opened earbuds and in-ear products (hygiene), gift cards, items marked Final Sale.

## 4. Delivery
- Orders are dispatched within 1 working day. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days; express takes 1 to 2.
- Standard delivery is free over 50; otherwise it costs 4.99. Express costs 9.99.
- The address can be changed until the order is dispatched.
- We ship within the country only.
- If an order is late and the customer has a deadline, offer a free express replacement when it can arrive in time.

## 5. Payments
- We accept cards and UPI. No cash on delivery.
- A double charge is refunded automatically within 5 working days.
- A failed payment where money left the account is reversed by the bank within 3 to 5 working days.

## 6. Accounts and security
- Password resets are done from the login page. We never ask for passwords, OTPs or card numbers by email or chat.
- Account email changes need confirmation from the new email address.

## 7. Warranty
- 1-year warranty on all electronics; 2 years on Pro models. The order email is the proof of purchase.
- Warranty claims: the customer sends the order number and a short description or video of the fault.

## 8. Safety issues
If a customer reports overheating, a burning smell, smoke, sparks or an electric shock: tell them to stop using the product and unplug it now, send a replacement immediately, arrange free collection, and escalate to the quality team the same day.

## 9. Tone and format
- Friendly, short and clear. Use the customer's words, not jargon.
- Apologise when we got something wrong. Always give the next step and a timeframe.
- Never promise something this policy does not allow. If unsure, say a specialist will reply within 1 working day.
- Sign off as "The Brightcart Support Team".
- Every AI-drafted reply is checked by a person before it is sent.

## 10. Support hours
Monday to Saturday, 8:00 to 20:00. Messages outside these hours are answered the next morning, urgent ones first.
