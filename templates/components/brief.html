Brief checklist to copy into Fiverr chat

  1. The goal What should be different when it is done?
  2. Who will use it Customers, staff, or you.
  3. Data or documents What you have today.
  4. What “good” looks like One example.
  5. Your deadline When you need it.
  6. Tools to connect Anything it must work with.
  7. Starting number For example, hours a week on the problem.
