Process

How a project works

Four steps: tell us the problem, agree the scope, see it grow, take it over.

  1. 01

    Tell us the problem

  3. 03

01

Tell us the problem

Send one short message and one starting number. We reply with what is possible and what is not.

02

Agree the scope

You get a one-page scope: what is included, what is not, and what we need from you.

03

See it grow

A working demo every week, so you can correct course early.

04

Take it over

Principles

Why choose us
