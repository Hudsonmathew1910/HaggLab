Try it pages

What happens when you try a project

  1. Step 1Sample data

    Each tool runs on built-in sample data, such as a sample handbook, sales file or product list.

  2. Step 2No file uploads

    You type or paste text. Text is limited in length and checked before use.

  3. Step 3Processed on the server

    The AI call runs on our server. Keys never reach your browser.

  4. Step 4Limited

    Each visitor can try up to 10 times an hour, to prevent misuse.

Please do not enter passwords or confidential information when you try a project.
